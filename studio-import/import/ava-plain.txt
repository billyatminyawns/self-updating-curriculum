In this walkthrough, you'll settle a total-loss auto claim in ClaimsCore, from valuation to payment. Start from the claim file.

From the left navigation, open the Payments tab. The settlement worksheet opens, with the vehicle valuation on the right.

From the left navigation, open the Settlement menu and choose Total Loss. The settlement worksheet opens, with the vehicle valuation on the right.

Confirm the actual cash value, then apply the deductible and any lienholder payoff. The net amount updates as you go.

If the settlement needs a supervisor's approval, select Hold for Review. The claim waits in the queue until your supervisor signs off.

If the settlement needs a supervisor's approval, select Pend. The claim waits in the queue until your supervisor signs off.

Review the salvage and title steps before you issue payment. Each step has to be acknowledged, or the payment button stays gray.

Once approved, the payment posts to the claim, and the customer receives a settlement letter automatically. That completes the walkthrough.
